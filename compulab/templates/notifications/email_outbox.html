"Sent" means the mail server accepted the message, not that it reached the inbox. If someone says an email never arrived, check their Spam folder and the sending mailbox for a "Mail Delivery Subsystem" bounce. Email (SMTP) cannot guarantee exactly-once delivery: if the connection drops right after the mail server accepted a message but before it confirmed, the outbox retries and the person may get the same email twice. Duplicate queueing of the same email is prevented; this rare double delivery is not. Email is separate from cloud sync: a sent email says nothing about whether data reached the cloud.
